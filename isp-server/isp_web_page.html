Network

Who gets in

The ISP asks for the strongest method allowed first; the guest may name another allowed one. MS-CHAP-2 needs accounts (it proves the ISP knows the password too).

Encryption (MPPE)

MPPE's keys come from MS-CHAP or MS-CHAP-2 with an account's password; required encryption needs accounts and one of those.

Compression (CCP)

The ISP takes the first of the guest's choices it allows. With MPPE, only MPPC rides along. Guest LAN, speed and throttle apply at once; the rest to the next call.
